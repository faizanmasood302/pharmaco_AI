# CPIC Guideline for SLCO1B1, ABCG2, and CYP2C9 and Statin-Associated Musculoskeletal Symptoms (2022 Update)

**Source:** Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium (CPIC)
**Authors:** Cooper-DeHoff RM, Niemi M, Ramsey LB, et al.
**Published:** Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics, 2022; 111(5):1007-1021
**PMID:** 35152405
**PMCID:** PMC9035072
**DOI:** 10.1002/cpt.2557

---

## 1. Overview

This guideline replaces the 2012 and 2014 CPIC guidelines for SLCO1B1 and simvastatin-induced myopathy. New additions include recommendations for CYP2C9 and ABCG2, and recommendations for all statins (simvastatin, atorvastatin, rosuvastatin, fluvastatin, pravastatin, pitavastatin, lovastatin).

**Genes:**
- **SLCO1B1** (OATP1B1 transporter) — hepatic uptake of all statins
- **ABCG2** (BCRP efflux transporter) — absorption and disposition of rosuvastatin
- **CYP2C9** (Phase I metabolizing enzyme) — oxidation of fluvastatin

## 4. Therapeutic Recommendations

### 4.1 SLCO1B1-Based Dosing (Table 2)

**All statins — SLCO1B1 Increased or Normal Function:**
- Prescribe desired starting dose and adjust based on disease-specific guidelines. (Strong)

**Simvastatin:**
| SLCO1B1 Phenotype | Recommendation | Strength |
|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Decreased function | Alternative statin. If simvastatin warranted, limit dose to <20 mg/day. | Strong |
| Poor function | Alternative statin. | Strong |

**Atorvastatin:**
| SLCO1B1 Phenotype | Recommendation | Strength |
|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Decreased function | Starting dose ≤40 mg. Aware of increased myopathy risk at 40 mg. If >40 mg needed, consider combination therapy. | Moderate |
| Poor function | Starting dose ≤20 mg. If >20 mg needed, consider rosuvastatin or combination therapy. | Moderate |

**Rosuvastatin:**
| SLCO1B1 Phenotype | Recommendation | Strength |
|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Decreased function | Desired starting dose. Aware of increased risk for doses >20 mg. | Strong |
| Poor function | Starting dose ≤20 mg. If >20 mg needed, consider combination therapy. | Moderate |

**Fluvastatin:**
| SLCO1B1 Phenotype | Recommendation | Strength |
|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Decreased function | Desired starting dose. Aware of increased risk for doses >40 mg/day. | Moderate |
| Poor function | Starting dose ≤40 mg/day. If >40 mg needed, consider alternative statin or combination therapy. | Moderate |

**Pravastatin:**
| SLCO1B1 Phenotype | Recommendation | Strength |
|-------------------|---------------|----------|
| Decreased function | Desired starting dose. Aware of increased risk for doses >40 mg/day. | Moderate |

**Pitavastatin:**

## 5. Statin Intensity and Alternative Selection (Figure 1)

| Intensity | Statins |
|-----------|---------|
| **High** | Atorvastatin 40-80 mg, Rosuvastatin 20-40 mg |
| **Moderate** | Atorvastatin 10-20 mg, Rosuvastatin 5-10 mg, Simvastatin 20-40 mg, Pravastatin 40-80 mg, Fluvastatin 40-80 mg, Pitavastatin 2-4 mg |
| **Low** | Simvastatin 10 mg, Pravastatin 10-20 mg, Fluvastatin 20-40 mg |

**Alternative statins (lowest SAMS risk) per SLCO1B1 phenotype:**
- SLCO1B1 Normal: all statins at standard doses
- SLCO1B1 Decreased: pravastatin, fluvastatin at ≤40 mg
- SLCO1B1 Poor: pravastatin ≤40 mg, fluvastatin ≤40 mg, atorvastatin ≤20 mg, rosuvastatin ≤20 mg

**Non-statin combination therapy:** ezetimibe

## 6. Safety Considerations

### Myopathy Risk
- SLCO1B1 poor function: 2-4x increased myopathy risk with simvastatin
- Graded relationship: poor function > decreased function > normal function
- Drug-drug interactions can exacerbate risk (e.g., gemfibrozil + statin)

### Asian Ancestry
- Rosuvastatin exposure 2-fold higher in Asian populations
- Consider lower starting doses independent of genotype

### Renal and Hepatic Function
- Dose limits based on renal and hepatic function should be evaluated prior to initiating statin therapy
- Drug-drug interaction effects may be more pronounced and result in higher myopathy risk
