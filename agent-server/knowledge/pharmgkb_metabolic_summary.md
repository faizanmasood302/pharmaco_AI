# PharmGKB Clinical Annotation: CYP2D6 + Opioids
**Source:** Pharmacogenomics Knowledgebase (PharmGKB)
**Evidence Level:** 1A (High Evidence)

## 1. Summary of Evidence
Allelic variation in CYP2D6 is a primary determinant of morphine production from codeine. The PharmGKB consensus validates the metabolic shunts involved in codeine pharmacology.

## 2. Metabolic Shunts
*   **CYP2D6 (10%):** O-demethylation to Morphine (Active).
*   **CYP3A4 (80%):** N-demethylation to Norcodeine (Inactive).
*   **UGT2B7 (10%):** Glucuronidation to Codeine-6-glucuronide.

## 3. Phenotypic Outcomes
*   **Ultrarapid:** Morphine concentrations are significantly higher than therapeutic ranges.
*   **Poor:** Morphine concentrations are below therapeutic limits for pain management.
*   **Intermediate:** May require dose adjustment but generally managed with standard ranges.
