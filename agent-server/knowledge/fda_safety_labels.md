# FDA Table of Pharmacogenomic Biomarkers in Drug Labeling
**Authority:** U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

## 1. Codeine Sulfate
### Black Box Warning
*   **Respiratory Depression:** Fatal cases of respiratory depression have occurred in children who were ultra-rapid metabolizers of codeine due to a CYP2D6 polymorphism.
*   **Post-Operative Risk:** Avoid in children following tonsillectomy or adenoidectomy.
*   **Breastfeeding:** Deaths have been reported in nursing infants whose mothers were ultra-rapid metabolizers.

## 2. Tramadol Hydrochloride
### Warning & Precautions
*   **CYP2D6 Interaction:** Tramadol's analgesic activity is primarily dependent on the O-desmethyl metabolite (M1), formed by CYP2D6.
*   **Genetic Variation:** Use in Ultra-Rapid Metabolizers is contraindicated due to increased risk of opioid toxicity.

## 3. General Guidance
*   The FDA recommends genetic testing for patients with prior history of opioid sensitivity or lack of response.
