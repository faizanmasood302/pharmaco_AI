# Override and Audit Policy
**Owner:** Clinical Director / Chief Medical Officer.

## 1. Override Eligibility
Clinicians may only override a **CRITICAL** or **HIGH** risk flag under the following conditions:
*   Patient has demonstrated tolerance to the agent in the past (History check required).
*   No viable non-PGx alternative exists for the specific indication.
*   The clinician provides a detailed rationale and accepts medical liability.

## 2. Mandatory Audit Fields
When an override is performed, the following data must be captured by the **Challenger Agent**:
*   `clinical_justification`
*   `monitoring_plan` (e.g., 24-hour respiratory check)
*   `patient_informed_consent`

## 3. Post-Prescription Surveillance
Any cleared prescription for an at-risk metabolizer MUST trigger a **72-hour Adherence Check-in** to monitor for adverse events.
