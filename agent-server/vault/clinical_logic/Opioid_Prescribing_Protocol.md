# Opioid Prescribing Protocol (PGx-Driven)
**Version:** 1.0 (2026 Standard)
**Scope:** CYP2D6 and CYP3A4-dependent analgesics.

## 1. Core Safety Rules (Deterministic)
*   **CYP2D6 Ultra-Rapid Metabolizers (UM):**
    *   **BLOCK:** Codeine, Tramadol.
    *   **Reasoning:** High risk of life-threatening toxicity due to rapid conversion to active metabolites (Morphine/O-desmethyltramadol).
*   **CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers (PM):**
    *   **BLOCK:** Codeine, Tramadol.
    *   **Reasoning:** Anticipated therapeutic failure; drug remains inactive.

## 2. Risk Escalation Matrix
| Risk Level | Enzyme Activity | Clinical Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CRITICAL** | UM + Prodrug | Immediate Block; switch to non-CYP2D6 agent. |
| **HIGH** | PM + Prodrug | Administrative Flag; treatment failure likely. |
| **MODERATE** | IM (Intermediate) | Consider 25% dose reduction or close monitoring. |

## 3. Preferred Alternatives
*   For neuropathic pain: **Pregabalin** or **Duloxetine**.
*   For acute pain: **NSAIDs** or non-CYP2D6 opioids (e.g., Hydromorphone).
